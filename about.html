Die Gryn is a pop artist, singer and songwriter based in Barcelona. Born in Buenos Aires and shaped by years in Copenhagen's club scene, he writes dark, sensual dance-pop about desire, heartbreak and the dance floor.

"Climax" opens a new era: a world where a baroque court and an underground club collide.
